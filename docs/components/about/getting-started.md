# Getting Started

To help you hit the ground running, follow these essential first steps to configure your account and start managing your practice effectively.

### 1. Log In & Dashboard Orientation 

Access the platform via your unique login URL. Upon signing in, you will land on the [**Home Dashboard**](../about/home). Treat this as your daily launchpad; it displays key business metrics at the top, followed by your daily agenda, task list, and pipeline summary.

### 2. Set Up Your Profile 
Navigate to your [**Profile Settings**](../about/user-profile). Ensure your personal details, and contact information are correct. This step is critical to ensure that your meeting scheduler and calendar syncs function accurately.

### 3. Connect Email & Calendar 
Navigate to the [**Emails**](../emails) module and select [**E-mail & Calendar Accounts**](../emails#e-mail--calendar-accounts). Follow the prompts to connect your professional account (Gmail or Outlook). This integration syncs your communications and enables the platform to automatically track client interactions.

### 4. Add Your First Contacts 
Head to the [**Contacts**](../contacts) module. You can add a new contact manually to familiarize yourself with the data fields.

:::tip TIP
When you [**add a Contact**](../contacts/contact-actions#how-to-add-a-new-contact), the system effectively [**auto-creates a Household**](../households/household-actions#how-to-create-a-household) record to link them to. Check the [**Households**](../households) module to see this connection in action.
:::

### 5. Explore the Modules 
Click through the [**main sidebar navigation**](../about/navigation#sidebar-menu) to visit [**Households**](../households), [**Opportunities**](../opportunities), [**Tasks**](../tasks), and [**Meetings**](../meetings). Understanding how these modules interconnect (*e.g., a Task linked to a Contact, which belongs to a Household*) is key to mastering the platform.
