# Platform Overview 

**Super Advisor** is a comprehensive Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and practice management platform engineered specifically for the unique complexities of wealth management. Built for authentic advisors who want to future-proof their practices, Super Advisor is a unified system that seamlessly orchestrates daily operations.

The platform is designed on the principle that technology should enhance, never replace, the human touch in financial advising. By eliminating fragmented tools and centralizing data, Super Advisor automates routine administrative tasks. This allows advisors to easily manage the intricate web of family members, legal entities, and financial assets that make up a client's total wealth. The system brings a client's entire financial life into one clear view—equipping you to be instantly better informed, fully prepared, and confident in delivering highly personalized, elite financial guidance.

## Key Capabilities & Modules

Super Advisor provides a custom suite of features specifically designed to cover essential wealth management processes:

* **CRM & Relationship Management:** Establish a "Golden Record" of truth to manage entire Households, tracking both individual contacts and complex family entities with custom fields for life stages and financial purposes.

* **Portfolio Management & Financial Aggregation:** Aggregate assets, liabilities, and external holdings in the Financial Records database to power holistic, data-driven financial advice.

* **Pipeline Tracking (Opportunities):** Forecast revenue, track deal stages (from Discovery to Proposal), and manage business growth with Gross vs. Adjusted pipeline views.

* **Workflows & Automation:** Standardize complex operations—like multi-step client transitions or onboarding—with the Workflows engine, automatically assigning tasks and drafting emails so nothing falls through the cracks.

* **Unified Communication & Meeting Scheduling:** Sync your Email and Calendar (Outlook/Gmail) directly to the platform. Create custom meeting templates and booking links that automatically generate Zoom conference details.

* **AI Assistant:** Utilize voice-to-text dictation to draft meeting notes, log tasks, and generate client emails simultaneously, right from the dashboard.

* **Digital Signatures & Custom Forms:** Send custom data-gathering questionnaires and execute agreements quickly and securely.

* **Document Vault:** Store, organize, and share important documents with clients securely.

* **Compliance & Service Adherence:** Track client engagement and ensure automated compliance with customized Service Models (e.g., meeting frequency for Gold vs. Bronze tiers).
