# Financial Records

The **Financial Records** module serves as the central repository for a client's entire financial life. From a project management perspective, this is your "source of truth" database. It is designed to consolidate fragmented financial data—ranging from liquid cash and public securities to complex private equity and liabilities—into a unified, real-time structure.

In holistic wealth management, data integrity is paramount. By linking every asset and liability to a specific Household, this module drives the calculation of Net Worth statements and informs strategic advice. It eliminates data silos, allowing you to see how a decision in one area (e.g., taking out a Line of Credit) impacts the overall financial picture (e.g., Net Worth and Cash Flow).

The **Financial Records** module is accessible from the sidebar, where each record type has its own dedicated page:

* [**Bank Accounts**](assets#bank-accounts) — Liquid cash holdings (checking, savings, money market).
* [**Investment Accounts**](investments#investment-accounts) — Brokerage, retirement accounts (IRAs, 401ks, RRSPs), and security holdings.
* [**Insurance Policies**](pensions#insurance-policies) — Life, Disability, and Critical Illness coverage contracts.
* [**Loans**](liabilities#loans) — Fixed-term debts (mortgages, personal loans, auto loans).
* [**Credit Cards**](liabilities#credit-cards) — Revolving consumer credit accounts.
* [**Lines of Credit**](liabilities#lines-of-credit) — Flexible borrowing instruments (HELOCs, business lines).
* [**Real Estate Properties**](assets#real-estate-properties) — Residential, commercial, and land holdings.
* [**Real Assets**](assets#real-assets) — Physical valuables (art, vehicles, jewelry, collectibles).
* [**Private Investments**](investments#private-investments) — Alternative holdings (venture capital, private equity, hedge funds).
* [**Company Equity**](investments#company-equity) — Stock options, RSUs, and equity compensation packages.
* [**Defined Benefit Pensions**](pensions#defined-benefit-pensions) — Employer pension plans and future income streams.
* [**Cash Flows**](pensions#cash-flows) — Income, expenses, and debt payments.

For detailed information on fields and forms for each record type, refer to the individual articles linked above.

:::tip
These Financial Records pages serve as the global registry for creating and managing individual records. To view these records in the context of a specific household—with visualizations, summary metrics, and categorized tables—use the [Financials section](../households/financials) within the Household profile.
:::
