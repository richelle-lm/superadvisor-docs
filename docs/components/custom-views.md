# Custom Views

## Overview

The **Custom Views** module serves as your personalization engine for managing lists across your platform, including **Contacts**, **Households**, **Tasks**, **Workflows**, **Notes**, **Meetings**, and **Financial Records**. It allows you to move beyond the default "All Records" display by creating tailored, saved views that surface exactly the information you need, when you need it. You can also customize your view directly from within individual records.

Instead of repeatedly applying the same filters every time you log in, Custom Views let you build specific, persistent dashboards—like "High Net Worth Clients", "Overdue Tasks", or "Upcoming Client Meetings". This feature empowers you to segment your book of business efficiently and focus your time on the data that matters most at any given moment.

### How to Customize the Contacts or Households List (Custom Views)

You can create personalized dashboard views to quickly access specific segments of your database (e.g., "High Priority Leads" or "NYC Clients").

1. Navigate to any supported module (*e.g., Contacts, Tasks, or Financial Records*) from the main sidebar.
2. **Configure your columns and filters:**
    * **Show/Hide:** Click the **Column Settings** button to check or uncheck standard and custom fields. This determines exactly what data is displayed.
    * **Adjust Layout:** Click the **Kebab Menu** (vertical dots) on any column header to **Sort** the data (Ascending/Descending), **Pin** the column to the side for easy scrolling, or Autosize the width.
    * **Add Filters:** Click the Filter icon on specific columns to define your criteria for this segment.
3. **Save your configuration:** Once you are ready to save your tailored layout, you will need to trigger the Save as New View pop-up. This pop-up appears when you do either of the following:
    * Click the View dropdown button at the top of the page and select Create New View.
    * Click the Save As New View button (which automatically appears at the top of the page once you start customizing an existing list on the fly).
:::note NOTE
If you simply want to update an existing custom view you are currently modifying, click the Save button instead.
:::

4. **Name your view:** In the **Save as New View** pop-up, enter a descriptive **View Name** and confirm to securely store your tailored layout.

### How to Apply Custom Views

Once created, these views become available throughout the platform:

1. Navigate to the main module where your view was created (*e.g., Households, Meetings, or Workflows*), or open the module directly from a record page.
2. Locate the **View Selector** dropdown.
3. Select your custom view (e.g., "VIP Clients") from the list.
4. The dashboard will instantly update to show only the records and columns defined in your custom configuration.
