# Super Mode

## Overview

**Super Mode** is a high-efficiency, multi-tabbed environment designed for multitasking. Unlike standard web pages that redirect you, **Super Mode** opens interactions (*Tasks, Contacts, Workflows*) in dynamic Tabs within the application. This allows you to work on multiple contexts—such as drafting an email while viewing a client profile—without losing your place.

The interface is anchored by the Explorer, which serves as your home dashboard.

## The Explorer

The **Explorer** is the default landing page of **Super Mode**. It is designed to be the starting point for all interactions.

* **Global Search:** Located in the center, this bar allows you to locate specific **Contacts**, **Households**, or **Notes**. Clicking a result opens it in a **New Tab**.
* **Managing Tabs:**
    * **Open:** Search results and **Quick Actions** automatically open in new tabs.
    * **Switch:** Click between the **Explorer** tab and active work tabs to switch contexts instantly without reloading.
    * **Close:** Click the "X" on any tab to dismiss that work item.
* **Quick Actions:** Located on the **Explorer** page, **Quick Actions** allow you to instantly initiate new work items without navigating away from your current context.
    * **Create Task:** Opens a "New Task" tab to assign to-dos and attach files. For detailed instructions on filling out the task fields, please refer to the [**How to Create a Task**](../components/tasks#how-to-create-a-task) section.
    * **Launch Workflow**: Opens a "New Workflow" tab to trigger automated standard operating procedures (e.g., Client Onboarding). For detailed instructions on configuring and launching workflows, please refer to the [**How to Launch a Workflow**](../components/workflows#how-to-launch-a-workflow) section.
    * **Create Note:** Opens a "New Note" tab for capturing meeting minutes or phone logs. For detailed instructions on creating and linking notes, please refer to the Notes article.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** section in **Super Mode** provides a centralized view of your schedule and pending meeting requests. It allows you to toggle between your active daily schedule and a list of requested meetings that require attention. 

The two views are:
* **Scheduled:** Displays your standard calendar view with all confirmed upcoming meetings and events, mirroring Outlook or Gmail.
* **Requested:** Displays a list of meeting invitations that have been initiated but not yet confirmed, allowing you to track status and follow up.

## AI Assistant

The **Assistant** section is your AI-powered co-pilot located within **Super Mode**. It features an "Ask anything" input field where you can type commands, use voice dictation, or attach files to execute complex workflows. It is designed to handle administrative work—such as uploading documents, drafting emails, or scheduling meetings—through natural language.

* **Commands:** Use the "Ask anything" field to type requests, use **Voice Dictation**, or **Attach Files** for processing.
* **Document Uploads:** Upload a file and instruct the Assistant to file it to a specific Household or Contact record.
* **Threads:** Manage your interaction history via the threads list in the upper right:
    * **Active:** Ongoing conversations.
    * **Waiting for Input:** Threads requiring your confirmation.
    * **Completed:** Finished tasks.

## Tasks

The **Tasks** section is a streamlined interface for managing your daily to-dos and delegating work. It focuses on two primary states: executing work assigned to you and assigning pending work to others.

The tasks are categorized into:

* **My Tasks:** A comprehensive list of work items currently assigned to your user profile. Clicking a task opens it in a new tab.
* **To Assign:** A list of tasks created but not yet allocated to a specific team member, used for distributing work.

## Communications

The **Communications** section acts as your command center for email correspondence. Unlike a traditional inbox that lists all messages, this view is filtered to prioritize action, sorting emails into pending replies, delegated messages, and works in progress.

The emails are categorized into:

* **Emails to Answer:** Filters for incoming messages from clients or external contacts that strictly require a response.
* **Assigned to Me:** Displays email threads delegated to you by other team members for internal hand-offs.
* **Draft Emails:** Collects messages started manually or generated by the AI Assistant that are awaiting review and sending.

## Meetings

The **Meetings** section serves as a command center for your client interactions. It organizes your schedule into four actionable categories, ensuring that no meeting is unprepared for and no follow-up is missed.

The meetings are categorized into:

* **Needs Preparation:** Flags upcoming meetings that lack agendas or necessary document reviews.
* **Waiting on Schedule:** Tracks invitations sent to clients that have not yet been booked.
* **Scheduled:** The standard list of confirmed upcoming events.
* **To Wrap Up:** Lists past meetings that require minutes, post-meeting tasks, or archiving.

## Contacts

The **Contacts** section serves as your categorized client directory. It categorizes all individuals in your system into distinct relationship stages, allowing you to quickly filter and locate specific groups.

The contacts are categorized into:

* **Leads:** Potential clients at the start of the relationship funnel.
* **Prospects:** Qualified leads currently in active engagement.
* **Onboarding:** Clients currently going through the account setup process.
* **Clients:** Active clients with established accounts and services.
* **Other:** Professional contacts, centers of influence, or non-client relationships.

## Workflows

The **Workflows** section displays a master list of **All Workflows** currently in progress across your firm. This view allows you to monitor the status of client onboarding, reviews, and other active pipelines.

Clicking a workflow opens it in a new tab, allowing you to view completed steps and execute current action items.

## Notes

The **Notes** section displays a simple, comprehensive list of **All Notes** recorded in the system. This allows you to quickly browse through historical meeting minutes and observations.

Clicking a note opens the full editor in a new tab, where you can read content or use @ mentions to link the note to specific entities.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** section features the **To See** category, which serves as your primary inbox for system alerts. It aggregates all new notifications in one list, ensuring you don't miss important updates.

Clicking a notification automatically opens the relevant Task, Workflow, or Record in a new tab for immediate action.
